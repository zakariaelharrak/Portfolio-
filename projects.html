Gemplaces: Plan Trips, Discover Places and Famous Views — App Case Study

Gemplaces is a platform designed to answer a common question: “Where should I go?” It helps users discover places based on their interests, activities, and preferences through search and filters. Users can also explore random places when they need inspiration. The platform includes an AI chatbot that suggests places and answers questions based on users’ needs. It also helps users create personalized plans and itineraries for their next adventure.

Nota: Notes and To-Do List Platform — UI/UX Case Study

Nota is a notes and task management platform designed to make daily organization easier. It improves the traditional notes and to-do list experience with useful features, including automation and AI tools. We conducted a survey to understand users’ needs, challenges, and expectations. The results helped us identify key problems and features that could improve the experience. Our goal is to create a simple and practical platform that helps users manage their notes and tasks more efficiently.

Chajara: Tree Donation App for a Greener World — UI/UX Case Study

Chajara is an app that helps individuals and businesses support a greener and more sustainable world. It connects users with authorized organizations working on tree planting and environmental protection. The platform makes donating easier and helps users support causes they care about. With a simple and user-friendly design, Chajara aims to make the donation process clear and accessible. Its mission is to protect nature, restore forests, and build a healthier planet for future generations.

Sonico: Smartphone Parts — UI/UX Case Study

Sonico is an online platform that makes buying mobile phone repair parts simple and convenient. Users can find essential components, including screens, batteries, and other replacement parts.
