The trophy

Winning First Place in My First Regional Infographics Competition

On April 17, 2024, I participated in my first infographics competition, bringing together over 30 participants from the Tangier-Tetouan-Al Hoceima region. Despite being a newcomer, I challenged myself, showcased my skills, and earned First Place. It was an unforgettable experience that boosted my confidence and motivated me to keep improving.

From Initial Ideas to the Final Design

I started by studying the specifications and understanding the subject before sketching my initial ideas. I then combined them into a cohesive design and presented it to mentors for feedback. Their advice helped me refine the sizing and colors, allowing me to deliver a design that communicated my ideas despite the limited time.
